Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how the iPhone game Slice the Shape ("the App") handles information. The App is made by an independent developer ("we").

What the App itself stores

Your level progress, stars, best score and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your device. We don't have accounts, servers or analytics of our own, and we don't receive this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped)
  • Diagnostic data about ad performance and crashes
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks

You decide about tracking: iOS asks for permission, and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you don't allow it, you'll still see ads, but they'll be less personalized.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads, and can change their choice any time in the App's Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through ads, contact us and we'll help.

Your rights

Because we don't store your personal data, there's nothing for us to delete; deleting the App removes all locally stored progress. For data processed by Google, use Google's tools linked above or your device's tracking settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
