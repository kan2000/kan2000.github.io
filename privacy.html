Color Tap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy for Color Tap: Brain Game ("the app") explains what information is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Information the app stores on your device

Best score and settings. Your best score and game settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, Google may collect and use:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • Your IP address and approximate location (country or region)
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • Information about ads you see and tap

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy Settings on the app's main menu. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

If the app offers purchases, they are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location, and we do not run our own servers or analytics.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
