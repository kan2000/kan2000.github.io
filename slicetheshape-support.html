How do I play?

Swipe once across the shape. The closer both pieces are to 50%, the better your score. Each level needs a few good cuts; a cut that's too far off costs a heart.

How do stars work?

Clear a level with an average under 1% off a perfect half for ⭐⭐⭐, under 2.5% for ⭐⭐, otherwise ⭐.

Can I turn off sound or music?

Yes — tap the gear icon on the main menu.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings (gear icon) → Ad Privacy Choices (shown where required, e.g. EU/UK). You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
